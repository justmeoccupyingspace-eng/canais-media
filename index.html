canais-dark

canais-dark is a small private tool that publishes and measures videos on the YouTube channels owned by its developer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does: uploads videos to the developer's own YouTube channels, reads those channels' own analytics, and stores the rendered video files in the developer's own Google Drive.
